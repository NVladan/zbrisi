# How to find cheap flights from the Balkans

The Balkans are a brilliant base for cheap travel around Europe. Low-cost carriers like **Wizz Air** and **Ryanair** have bases in the region and routinely sell one-way fares for the price of a nice dinner. Here's how to make the most of it.

## Where to start

The most low-cost routes leave from:

- [Belgrade (BEG)](/en/flights/BEG) — a Wizz Air base with the widest choice
- [Sarajevo (SJJ)](/en/flights/SJJ)
- [Skopje (SKP)](/en/flights/SKP)
- [Zagreb (ZAG)](/en/flights/ZAG)
- [Podgorica (TGD)](/en/flights/TGD) and [Tivat (TIV)](/en/flights/TIV)

## Three rules for the lowest price

1. **Stay flexible on dates.** A few days either way can halve the price — which is why Zbriši searches the cheapest days across the whole month.
2. **Travel hand-luggage only.** The headline fare is the base fare (a small under-seat bag); anything more is an add-on.
3. **Compare everything in one place.** Instead of opening every airline site, [search Zbriši](/en/) and get the cheapest fares across all of them at once.

## Where's cheapest?

Germany, Italy and Austria are especially good value from the region:

- [Belgrade → Vienna](/en/flights/BEG/VIE)
- [Sarajevo → Rome](/en/flights/SJJ/ROM)
- [Zagreb → Barcelona](/en/flights/ZAG/BCN)

## When to book

For flights within Europe, the sweet spot is **3–8 weeks before departure**, mid-week. For seasonal destinations, book earlier.
