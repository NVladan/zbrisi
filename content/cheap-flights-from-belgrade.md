# Cheap flights from Belgrade

Belgrade (BEG) is the busiest hub in the western Balkans, which is good news for your wallet: Air Serbia's home base plus low-cost carriers mean there's almost always a cheap one-way fare to somewhere in Europe.

## Which airlines fly from Belgrade

- **Air Serbia** — the widest network, frequent promotions across Europe
- **Wizz Air** — low-cost links to Germany, Scandinavia and Western Europe
- **Ryanair** — seasonal budget routes
- Legacy carriers (Lufthansa, Turkish, Austrian, Pegasus) for onward connections

## How to get the lowest fare

1. **Stay flexible on dates.** A few days either way can halve the price — so [Zbriši](/en/) searches the cheapest days across the whole month by default.
2. **Fly hand-luggage only** on Wizz Air and Ryanair to avoid add-on fees.
3. **Book mid-week.** Fares set on Tuesday–Thursday are often lower than weekend prices.
4. **Compare one-way both ways.** Two separate one-ways are sometimes cheaper than a return.

## When to book

For flights within Europe, the sweet spot is **3–8 weeks before departure**. For summer and holiday travel, book earlier — the cheapest seats sell first.

## Common questions

**How much is a cheap flight from Belgrade?** Short European one-ways from Belgrade regularly start around €20–€40 on low-cost carriers when you're flexible on dates.
