# Cheap flights from Zagreb

Zagreb (ZAG) has become much cheaper to fly from since Ryanair opened a base there — low-cost competition with Croatia Airlines means more routes and lower one-way fares across Europe.

## Which airlines fly from Zagreb

- **Ryanair** — the growing low-cost network, the source of most bargain fares
- **Croatia Airlines** — the national carrier, wide European coverage
- **Wizz Air** and legacy carriers (Lufthansa, Austrian, Turkish) for more options and connections

## How to get the lowest fare

1. **Be flexible on dates** — [Zbriši](/en/) hunts the cheapest days across the whole month by default.
2. **Hand-luggage only** on Ryanair and Wizz to skip baggage fees.
3. **Book mid-week and early** for summer and holiday travel.

## When to book

The sweet spot for European routes is **3–8 weeks before departure**, mid-week. Seasonal sun routes sell out earlier.

## Common questions

**How much is a cheap flight from Zagreb?** Flexible one-ways within Europe often start around €20–€40 on low-cost carriers.
