# Cheap flights from Sarajevo

Sarajevo (SJJ) is a great base for cheap travel around Europe. Low-cost carriers keep adding routes, and one-way fares often cost about as much as a nice dinner out.

## Which airlines fly from Sarajevo

- **Wizz Air** — the widest low-cost network, especially to Germany, Italy and Scandinavia
- **Ryanair** — seasonal links to Western Europe
- Legacy carriers (Pegasus, Turkish, Austrian) for onward connections

## Three rules for the lowest price

1. **Stay flexible on dates.** A few days either way can halve the price — which is why [Zbriši](/en/) searches the cheapest days across the whole month by default.
2. **Travel hand-luggage only** on low-cost airlines to dodge add-on fees.
3. **Book early** — the cheapest summer and holiday seats disappear fast.

## When to book

For flights within Europe, the sweet spot is **3–8 weeks before departure**, mid-week. For seasonal destinations, book earlier.
