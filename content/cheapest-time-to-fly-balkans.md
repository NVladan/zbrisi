# The cheapest time to fly from the Balkans

The same route can cost twice as much depending on **when** you travel and **when** you buy. Here's how to land on the right side of that gap.

## Cheapest months

Off-season, prices drop. For most European destinations the best value is:

- **Late autumn** (October–November)
- **Early winter** (outside the holidays)
- **Late January and February**

Summer (July–August) and the holidays are the priciest — if you must travel then, book well ahead.

## Cheapest days of the week

- **Departing**: Tuesday, Wednesday and Saturday are usually cheaper than Friday or Sunday.
- **Buying**: there's no magic day, but mid-week tends to have more cached low fares than the weekend.

## How far ahead to buy

- **Europe (short hops)**: the sweet spot is **3–8 weeks** before departure.
- **Seasonal & holiday routes**: 2–4 months ahead — the cheapest seats go first.
- **Last minute**: occasional deals exist, but it's a gamble.
