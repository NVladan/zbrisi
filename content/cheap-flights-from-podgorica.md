# Cheap flights from Podgorica

Podgorica (TGD) is Montenegro's main gateway. Between the national carrier and seasonal low-cost flights, there are reliably cheap one-way fares to central and Western Europe — with more choice in the summer.

## Which airlines fly from Podgorica

- **Air Montenegro** — the national carrier, the backbone of the schedule
- **Ryanair** and **Wizz Air** — seasonal low-cost routes (strongest in summer)
- **Turkish, Austrian** and others for connections

## How to get the lowest fare

1. **Be flexible on dates** — [Zbriši](/en/) checks the cheapest days across the whole month.
2. **Travel light** on the low-cost carriers to avoid extra fees.
3. **Book early for summer** — Montenegro is a popular season, so seats and fares move fast.

## Common questions

**When is it cheapest to fly from Podgorica?** Outside the summer peak — spring and autumn fares are noticeably lower. See the [cheapest time to fly](/en/guides/cheapest-time-to-fly-balkans) guide.

**Are there low-cost flights from Podgorica?** Yes — mostly seasonal Ryanair and Wizz Air routes; the rest of the year, Air Montenegro promotions are the best bet. [Browse all routes](/en/flights/TGD).
