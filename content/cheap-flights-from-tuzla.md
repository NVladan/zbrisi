# Cheap flights from Tuzla

Tuzla (TZL) punches far above its size: it's a **Wizz Air base**, so a small airport delivers some of the lowest one-way fares anywhere in the Balkans — especially to Germany and Scandinavia.

## Which airlines fly from Tuzla

- **Wizz Air** — runs almost the entire schedule; this is where the cheap fares come from
- Seasonal charters for a handful of sun routes

Because it's effectively a single-airline airport, comparing dates matters even more — the same route can swing a lot week to week.

## How to get the lowest fare

1. **Be flexible on dates** — on a Wizz base, a day or two can move the price a lot; [Zbriši](/en/) checks the whole month.
2. **Travel hand-luggage only** and add bags only if you truly need them.
3. **Book early** — popular German routes fill up before holidays.

## Common questions

**Why is Tuzla so cheap?** A dedicated Wizz Air base keeps base fares low, and the airport has low charges — savings that reach the ticket price.

**Where can I fly from Tuzla?** Mostly Germany, Scandinavia, Switzerland and a few other Western European cities. [Browse all routes](/en/flights/TZL).
