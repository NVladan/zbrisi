# Cheap flights from Skopje

Skopje (SKP) is another Balkan **Wizz Air base**, which means consistently low one-way fares to Western and central Europe — often cheaper than flying from much larger cities nearby.

## Which airlines fly from Skopje

- **Wizz Air** — the largest network and the cheapest fares
- **Pegasus, Turkish, Austrian, Lufthansa** — for connections beyond Europe

## How to get the lowest fare

1. **Stay flexible on dates** — [Zbriši](/en/) searches the cheapest days across the whole month.
2. **Fly hand-luggage only** on Wizz Air to keep the headline fare.
3. **Book a few weeks ahead**, mid-week, for the best prices.

## Common questions

**How cheap are flights from Skopje?** Flexible one-ways to Germany and central Europe frequently start around €20–€35 on Wizz Air.
